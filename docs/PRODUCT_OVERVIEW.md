# Gunita

A historical discovery game: read a clue, solve an interactive challenge, and discover the story behind your answer. Choose roulette for mixed challenges, Where only for ten map rounds, or When only for ten timeline rounds.

The founding collection contains five Philippine and five other world events. Historical content retains its real people and place names; branding and interface labels are English. Games have ten rounds, repeating events if the selected pool has fewer than ten. Quick responses can earn a bounded bonus, but there is no answer deadline.

Routes: `/` discovery, `/play` setup, `/game` active expedition, `/results` statistics, `/archive` searchable content and sources, `/auth` accounts, `/admin` secure content management. Unknown routes show a recovery page. Guests can play every mechanic without registration.

The interface uses cool neutral surfaces, blue accents, larger sans-serif type and English category names. The brand is simply **Gunita**. Portrait choices and framing avoid obvious occupational cues where suitable historical imagery exists. There are no paid APIs, Google Maps, or Laravel dependencies.
