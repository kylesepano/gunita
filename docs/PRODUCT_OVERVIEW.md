# Gunita

A historical discovery game: read a clue, solve an interactive challenge, and discover the story behind your answer. Choose roulette for mixed challenges, or select Who, Where, When, What, Why or How for ten focused rounds.

The starter bank contains 124 Philippine and world events spanning ancient history through 2020. Historical content retains its real people and place names; branding and interface labels are English. Games have ten rounds, repeating events if the selected pool has fewer than ten. Who choices are drawn from people born within 100 years of the correct person. Quick responses can earn a bounded bonus, but there is no answer deadline.

Routes: `/` discovery, `/play` setup, `/game` active expedition, `/results` statistics, `/archive` searchable content and sources, `/auth` accounts, `/admin` secure content management. Unknown routes show a recovery page. Guests can play every mechanic without registration.

The interface uses cool neutral surfaces, blue accents, larger sans-serif type and English category names. The brand is simply **Gunita**. Portrait choices and framing avoid obvious occupational cues where suitable historical imagery exists. There are no paid APIs, Google Maps, or Laravel dependencies.
